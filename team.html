About Haus
A home for biology’s outsiders.
Haus is a pre-seed biotech fund. It catches founders before anyone else will — pre-incorporation, inside the house — and writes the first check. Biopunk Ventures, the operating company, runs the accelerator: co-living residencies, shared wet labs, and the community that turns a lab idea into a company. A small team and a wide network.
