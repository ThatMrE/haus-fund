Build the house with us.
Haus is a small team running a pre-seed fund and the Biopunk accelerator behind it — houses, labs, and a community. These are the roles we’re hiring for now.
Community Architect
San Francisco · Full-time, live-in
  • Own end-to-end house operations — logistics, vendors, safety, and the systems that make shared living frictionless.
  • Run the full resident lifecycle: sourcing and vetting applicants, onboarding, and offboarding.
  • Design and execute the calendar of dinners, salons, workshops, and demos that create cross-pollination.
  • Connect the house to the wider Haus ecosystem — events, lab, fund, and network — and surface investment-relevant signals.
  • Steward the norms, rituals, and culture that make the house worth belonging to.
Compensation is founder-adjacent: full room and board, carry in the companies you shepherd, and cash comp tied to sponsorship you bring in. Reports directly to the Haus Fund partners.

Sponsorship Coordinator
Non-exclusive · Contract
  • Prospect and qualify sponsors aligned with the target categories — platform, capital, and ecosystem partners.
  • Run outreach, discovery conversations, and pitches using approved materials and pricing.
  • Develop deals: negotiate sponsorship structure, value, and terms within approved parameters, then facilitate closing.
  • Keep the pipeline current in the CRM, report at least monthly, and hand closed sponsors cleanly to the team for fulfillment.
Non-exclusive independent contractor. Sponsorships fund the Biopunk program’s operating costs; cash compensation scales with what you close. No authority to bind the company.

Events Lead
San Francisco · Contract
  • Own the calendar of in-person Haus surfaces — Demo Days, Midpoint Pitches, and the weekly founder dinners.
  • Produce the SF Biohackathon (annual, 250+), the monthly Wetware Meetup, and Kill-Your-Baby sessions.
  • Run partner-facing roadshows and sponsor-led roundtables.
  • Make the houses feel like a movement, not a co-working space.
Independent contractor, twelve-month term.

House Manager
Per house · Contract
  • Run the physical surface of a house — cohort move-in and move-out, keys, welcome kits, orientation.
  • Manage lab supplies and BSL-1/2 safety in coordination with the on-site lab lead.
  • Coordinate building maintenance, vendors, and the weekly Tuesday dinner.
  • Be the resident liaison — housing issues, conflict mediation, escalation to the Community Architect.
  • Own security and access — key cards, after-hours protocol, and guest-policy enforcement.
One per house, twelve-month term — the difference between co-living and a community.

Operations Manager
San Francisco · Full-time
  • Run the day-to-day machinery of the operating company — bookkeeping, AP/AR, monthly close, board financials.
  • Manage banking and payments — accounts, card spend, vendor payments, founder SAFE wires.
  • Own vendor management and compliance — legal, insurance, payroll, state filings, K-1s.
  • Keep the operating-company / fund interface clean: program costs in Biopunk Ventures, investments through Haus Ventures Fund.
Full-time. Reports to the General Partner and works alongside the Community Architect.

Media Lead
Remote · Contract
  • Own Haus's public voice — social, newsletter, press, and founder stories.
  • Ship the newsletter twice a month with a founder spotlight in every issue; post 4–6× a week on X and LinkedIn and amplify residents daily.
  • Run Instagram in a documentary register, a weekly Discord digest, and at least one founder-led video per cohort.
  • Handle press and media relations for partnership announcements, and hold the brand line across every Haus surface.
Independent contractor, calibrated to the brand guide. Manifesto over marketing.

Venture Scout
Remote · Network role
  • Find breakthrough biotech founders pre-incorporation — the ones hidden in labs, PhD programs, Discord servers, and community-bio spaces.
  • Submit prospects through the Scout Portal before any other channel logs them — first touch, with the founder’s consent to be contacted.
  • Include the basics: name, contact, scientific area, affiliation, and your basis of relationship; the GP confirms first-touch status within five business days.
  • Earn scout carry on the investments your referrals become, per the Venture Scout Agreement.
A network role, not a salaried one — a 24-month engagement for connectors who already know where the next founders are hiding.
