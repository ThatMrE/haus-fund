The event
An all-day walk into 2050
The 2050 Final Showcase brings together innovators, researchers, founders, and visionaries to present the ideas that will shape the next quarter-century. Mabuhay Gardens is turned into a future-forward experience space: two stages of TED-style programming running concurrently, wrapped around a curated zone of immersive, interactive booths.

The first edition
Biopunk 2050: S26 Final Showcase ran at Mabuhay Gardens on 15 August 2026 under the theme “2050: Visions of an Optimistic Future” — the first time the room was built. What follows is the venue design brief for the full-scale build: the layout, the production spec, and what exhibitors are held to.
Venue layout
Two stages, one exhibition floor
Programming runs concurrently on both stages with separate audio feeds. The booth zone sits between them, so the walk from one talk to the next goes through the exhibition rather than around it.
Stage 1
Main stage — keynotes & featured talks
  • Configuration — theater-style seating, 200–300 capacity.
  • Dimensions — 24ft wide × 16ft deep × 2ft elevated.
  • Backdrop — full-width LED wall (16:9 or 32:9 ultrawide) on the navy/teal branded template.
  • Podium — minimal clear acrylic lectern with embedded confidence monitor.
  • Wings — black drape with teal edge-lighting for speaker entry and exit.
  • Audience — tiered or raked seating preferred; front rows held for speakers and VIPs.
Stage 2
Breakout stage — panels, lightning talks, demos
  • Configuration — flexible seating, 80–120 capacity.
  • Dimensions — 16ft wide × 12ft deep × 1ft elevated.
  • Backdrop — large-format screen (120″ minimum) with branded lower-third template.
  • Setup — modular; shifts from single speaker to panel (four chairs and a moderator) to demo table.
  • Intimacy — closer audience proximity, first row within 6ft of the stage.

Attendee journey
Twelve hours, one arc
Lighting and sound move with the day — warm in the morning, cool through the afternoon, dynamic in the evening. The venue should feel like it is keeping time with the programme.
10:00 · ARRIVAL
Street to 2050
A branded 20ft entry tunnel with projected ambient visuals and spatial audio makes the transition. Check-in prints a digital badge carrying a QR code for the schedule, booth map, and networking. The ambient soundscape shifts as you cross into the main space.
10:30–13:00 · MORNING
Opening keynote and featured talks
Stage 1 runs the opening keynote plus three to four featured talks at 20 minutes each. Stage 2 runs lightning talks at 8 minutes on a rolling schedule. Booths are open for exploration between talks.
13:00–14:30 · MIDDAY
Networking lunch
Lunch in the F&B zone. Booths stay open for extended demos; the slot is built for informal meetups and 1:1 connections rather than programming.
14:30–17:30 · AFTERNOON
Panels, demos, peak floor traffic
Stage 1 turns to panel discussions and featured talks; Stage 2 runs interactive demos and audience Q&A. This is peak booth traffic — every exhibitor staffed.
18:00–22:00 · EVENING
Closing keynote and reception
Lighting shifts to a warmer, more dynamic palette. Stage 1 carries the closing keynote and finale; Stage 2 becomes open mic and community showcase. A networking reception runs with music, food, and continued booth access.
Production spec
What has to be in the building
Both stages run independent programming, which means independent audio. Everything on Stage 1 is captured at podcast quality; the room is wired for 500 concurrent devices before a single exhibitor plugs in.
Audio-visual
  • Stage 1 — LED wall (12ft × 7ft minimum), front-fill plus line array PA, 2 wireless lavs, 2 handhelds, confidence monitor, presentation clicker.
  • Stage 2 — large-format screen and projector (6,000 lumens minimum), powered speakers, 2 wireless lavs, 1 handheld, stage monitor.
  • Both — separate audio feeds for independent programming, house mix position.
Lighting
  • Stage 1 — front wash in warm white, key light with adjustable colour temperature, teal and cyan edge accents, soft audience wash.
  • Stage 2 — simpler rig: front wash and two moving heads for colour accents.
  • Booth zone — warm ambient overhead wash with adjustable individual booth spots.
  • Venue-wide — projected patterns synced to the phases of the day: warm morning, cool afternoon, dynamic evening.
Connectivity & power
  • Dedicated event Wi-Fi for 500 concurrent devices, with a separate SSID for exhibitors.
  • Hardwired ethernet to every Immersive Pod and each AV position.
  • 200A total event load, dedicated circuits per booth row.
  • Four charging stations with USB-A, USB-C, and wireless.
Recording & streaming
  • Multi-camera capture on Stage 1 — two fixed, one roaming.
  • Single fixed camera on Stage 2.
  • Live stream capability over RTMP to YouTube or a custom platform.
  • Audio board feed for podcast-quality recording of every talk.

For exhibitors
What every booth is held to
The zone only reads as one exhibition if everyone builds to the same rules. These are the non-negotiables.
Header signage
Use the provided template — navy background, white text, teal accent bar. No substitutions.
Height limit
No structures above 8ft. Immersive Pods are exempt.
Sound
Audio must stay inside the booth footprint. Loud demos run on headphones.
Lighting
No strobes, no high-intensity floods. Booth spots are provided.
Setup window
The day before the event, 12:00–20:00.
Teardown
The same night by 00:00, or the following morning by 10:00.

Load-in / load-out
  • Load-in two days out for AV and staging; one day out for booths and decor.
  • Load-out the night of the event for exhibitors, next morning for AV and staging.
  • Loading dock dimensions and weight limits confirmed with the venue in advance.
Safety & accessibility
  • ADA-compliant pathways throughout, 36″ minimum clear width.
  • Emergency exits marked and unobstructed.
  • Fire marshal walkthrough four hours before doors.
  • First aid station staffed throughout.
  • A quiet room available for sensory breaks.
Staffing
  • 1 event manager, 2 stage managers, 3–4 AV techs, 3–4 registration staff.
  • Venue liaison, security, and F&B coordination.
  • A volunteer corps for wayfinding and attendee support.
