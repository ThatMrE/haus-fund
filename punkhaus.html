The node · PunkHaus
The flagship node
San Francisco is where the Haus model was proven. Two houses, a working community lab, and a cohort already in residence.
T
Terrain
The SF Bay Area is the densest AI-and-biotech cluster on earth. Frontier Tower, a 16-floor building in SoMa, gathers labs and frontier-AI teams a short walk from the houses.
L
Lab
The BioPunk community lab moved its centrifuges, microscopes, and benches onto Frontier Tower's biotech floor in April 2025. It is a separate building a short walk from the house — residents walk down the street to the bench.
H
Houses
Four houses run in the city, all in a neighborhood format — co-living, co-working, communal kitchen, dry-lab and wet-lab workspace across a few blocks rather than one address.
C
Community
Office hours with mentors. Community dinners weekly. Frontier Tower alone runs 3+ events a day across AI, biotech, robotics, and longevity.

Frontier Tower's neighbours are frontier-AI teams, robotics builders, and longevity researchers. The collisions are the point.
