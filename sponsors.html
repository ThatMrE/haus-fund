FAQ
Common questions.
What kinds of companies sponsor Haus?+
Life-science platforms, instrument and reagent brands, cloud and compute providers, CROs, service firms, funds, and frontier-tech companies that want direct access to biotech founders and investors. We tailor packages to your goal — brand, pipeline, dealflow, or community.
Can we sponsor a single event?+
Yes. Many partners start with one flagship moment — a Final Showcase or Reverse Demo Day — then expand into a series or title partnership once we align on format and audience.
What deliverables are included?+
Packages typically include logo placement, on-site presence, team passes, social and newsletter promotion, and optional speaking or activation slots. We share a custom one-pager after intake based on your tier and goals.
Can we partner across multiple nodes?+
Absolutely. The Haus network is expanding to global nodes — Kobe, Paris, Monterrey, Melbourne, and more. Title and series partners can activate across several houses at once, or focus on a single region.
How quickly will we hear back?+
We review every sponsor inquiry personally. If there’s a fit with an upcoming program, expect a reply within a few business days with next steps — no mass outreach.
