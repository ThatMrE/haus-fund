The opportunity
A market mispricing
Women are the most systematically underfunded group in venture — despite outperforming. Capital flows to pedigree and pattern-matching, not to performance. That gap is the arbitrage FemHaus is built to capture.
~2%
of U.S. venture capital goes to all-women founding teams
12%
of U.S. biotechs over $100M market cap have a female CEO
8%
of biotechs with early raises since late 2023 were women-led
The evidence
Why women are a better investment
The performance data is not close.
+63%
Companies with a female founder outperformed their all-male counterparts over a 10-year period.
+10%
Women-led startups generate 10%+ more cumulative revenue after five years than all-male teams — on less capital.
~2×
Women-owned businesses grew at nearly twice the rate of male-owned businesses between 2019 and 2023.
Higher
Firms with female CEOs and CFOs have shown higher stock performance than those led by men.
The category
Why biotech is the right category
The talent is already there. The leadership and the science are not — yet. That is the entry point.
60%
of the biology workforce is women.
20–30%
hold C-level roles in the field.
$1T
annual global upside from closing the women’s health gap (McKinsey, by 2040).
A structural data gap is a structural opportunity. Women are underrepresented in clinical trials, and there is a lack of research built around female biology. The femtech market alone is growing near 20% a year toward $50B+. Women founders see these problems first — and are best positioned to build the companies that solve them.
